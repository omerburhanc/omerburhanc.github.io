About me

The short version: I like understanding how things work, and then making them work better.

I think in forces, systems and people. Mechanical engineering is where all three meet.

I grew up in Gaziantep, a city of workshops and factories in southeastern Turkey, and I study at TOBB Science High School, a science-focused public school. Physics and mathematics are the core of my day. Outside class I build and fly model rockets, write software, and spend a lot of time in Model United Nations, where I went from representing China as a delegate to chairing the World Health Organization committee.

When I travel, I end up staring at the structure of a place: how a 17th-century palace in Amsterdam stands on wooden piles, how a Roman library façade carries its load, how the Atomium holds nine steel spheres in the air. This site collects those projects, notes and memories in one place.

Project 01 · Rocketry

Model rocket launch

Flown · Sept 2024Solid motorParachute recovery

A single-stage model rocket, launched from a rod pad on open ground outside the city. The video shows the full flight: ignition, the boost phase, coast to apogee, ejection, and the parachute bringing the rocket back down in one piece.

Getting a rocket to come back safely is the real engineering problem. The ejection charge has to fire close to apogee. Too early and the parachute opens at high speed and can tear; too late and the rocket is already falling fast. The Launch Lab below lets you explore exactly that trade-off.

// Forces each 2 ms: thrust along the rod/velocity, quadratic drag, gravity.
const vRelX = vx - wind, vRelY = vy;           // air-relative velocity
const v = Math.hypot(vRelX, vRelY);
const CdA = chuteOpen ? Cd_body*A_body + Cd_chute*A_chute : Cd_body*A_body;
const D = 0.5 * rho * CdA * v * v;               // drag force, N
const T = t < burnTime ? impulse / burnTime : 0; // average thrust, N
const m = dryMass + motorCase + propellant * (1 - t / burnTime);
ax = (T * dirX - D * vRelX / v) / m;
ay = (T * dirY - D * vRelY / v) / m - 9.81;
vx += ax * dt;  vy += ay * dt;  x += vx * dt;  y += vy * dt;
